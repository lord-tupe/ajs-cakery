Baking Happiness Since 2009

What started as a small kitchen experiment has blossomed into a beloved cakery in Blantyre. We believe that every cake and cupcake should be a little moment of joy — baked with the finest organic ingredients, crafted by hand, and decorated with love.

Our recipes have been perfected over 15 years, combining traditional techniques with creative modern flavors. From classic vanilla to exotic matcha, there's a treat for every occasion.
